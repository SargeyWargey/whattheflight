Facts, artwork and screenshots for writing about What The Flight?

What The Flight? shows every aircraft in the sky around you, live, on a radar that turns as you turn. Raise your iPhone and Sky View labels the planes you can see. Every one that passes goes in your logbook. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple TV, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

About the app

Hear a jet overhead and wonder where it’s going? The nearest aircraft takes the center of the screen: its flight number, airline, aircraft type, where it’s from and where it’s going, what it’s doing, its altitude, speed and distance. Every other aircraft sits on the edge of the screen in its real compass direction, nose pointing the way it’s flying, so whatever is at the top is in front of you.

Sky View opens the camera and labels the aircraft you can actually see. Take a photo and it’s filed as a boarding pass. Alerts give a heads-up before an aircraft passes overhead or when something unusual is around, and Sky Watch keeps watching from the Lock Screen for up to two hours. The logbook keeps every aircraft that comes by, with stats, records and 46 badges.

On Apple Watch, point your arm at a plane to hear what it is. On Apple TV, put a radar, a map, or both on the big screen. On iPad and the iPhone Duo, a live map sits beside the radar.
