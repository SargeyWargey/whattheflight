Version 1.0 Coming soon

The first release, for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple TV.

iPhone and iPad

  • A radar that turns as you turn: the nearest aircraft in the center, every other one on the edge in its real direction, and what each is doing, from climbing out to on approach.
  • Details for any aircraft: a photo of that exact airframe, its route on a map, how far along it is and when it passes closest to you.
  • Search the world by flight number, callsign or tail number. Pin up to five to keep them on your radar’s edge, and favorite the ones you love to see them in yellow.
  • Sky View: raise your iPhone to label the aircraft you can see, lock on, zoom and take a photo filed as a boarding pass.
  • Alerts for aircraft passing overhead and for notable traffic, and Sky Watch to keep watching from your Lock Screen for up to two hours.
  • A logbook of every aircraft that comes inside your radius, with stats for each place you watch from, records and 46 badges, synced with iCloud.
  • A live map beside the radar on iPad and on the iPhone Duo half open, four topographic backgrounds, ten app icons, widgets, Siri and metric units.

Apple Watch

  • A radar on your wrist with its own GPS.
  • Identify: point your arm at an aircraft to hear what it is. Find: haptics guide you to the one you picked.
  • Complications for the nearest aircraft, the whole sky and the next one overhead.

Apple TV

  • A radar, a map, or both side by side, facing the way your TV does.
  • Send your spot from your iPhone in one tap, or search for a place on the TV.
  • Alerts read aloud, the shared logbook, and nearby traffic on the Top Shelf.
